Echoes of Eden: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Echoes of Eden ("the app") is a virtual reality game for Meta Quest, made by KyleTheUnityDev ("I", "me").

What the app collects

The app does not collect, store, sell or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no online features. It does not need an internet connection to play.

Hand tracking

The app is played with your hands. Hand tracking is provided by your Meta Quest headset. The positions of your hands are used on the headset, while you play, so you can interact with the game. The app does not record them, store them or send them to me or to anyone else.

Microphone and other device permissions

The app does not use your microphone, camera or location. Some permissions listed for the app come from Meta and Unity software libraries it is built with. The app itself does not use them to collect any information about you.

Data handled by Meta

Your use of your Meta Quest headset and the Meta Horizon Store is covered by Meta's own privacy policy: https://www.meta.com/legal/privacy-policy/.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

For questions about this policy, please open an issue at github.com/kyle789xbl/echoes-of-eden/issues.
